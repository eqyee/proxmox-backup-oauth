Privacy Policy

Proxmox Backup is a private, single-user application used by its owner to transfer personal backups to Google Drive using rclone.

The application accesses Google Drive only for the purpose of creating and managing the owner's backup files.

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or collected by any external service operated by the application owner.

OAuth credentials and tokens used by rclone are stored locally on the owner's system.
